Privacy Policy

App: UFO Spaceboards VR
Effective date: 2026-02-07
Contact: politrons@gmail.com

1) Summary

UFO Spaceboards VR does not store, transmit, or sell personal information. The game stores your score locally on your headset and uses the Meta Horizon Platform to display player names on in-game leaderboards.

2) Data We Collect

We do not collect or store personal data such as email, precise location, or contacts. The following data is accessed through the Meta Platform:

  • User Profile Display Name (USER_PROFILE): We retrieve the display name of players who appear on the in-game leaderboard via the Meta Horizon Leaderboard API. This data is fetched in real-time and is not stored on the device or any external server.
  • Local-only data: High score / progress stored on the device (headset) only.
  • No analytics/ads SDKs: We do not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

3) How We Use Data

Local-only score/progress is used solely to display your results inside the game.

User Profile Display Names are used exclusively to show player identities next to their scores on the in-game leaderboard. This data is:

  • Fetched on-demand from the Meta Horizon Leaderboard API during gameplay.
  • Displayed temporarily in the leaderboard UI.
  • Not stored on the device, on any server, or in any database.
  • Not shared with any third parties.

4) Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties. Display names are only shown within the in-game leaderboard and are never transmitted outside of the game.

5) Data Retention

Scores and progress remain on your headset until you uninstall the game or clear the app data.

User Profile Display Names are not retained. They exist only in volatile memory during the active game session and are discarded when the leaderboard view is closed or the game exits.

6) Data Deletion

Since we do not store any User Profile data, there is no persistent data to delete on our end. If you wish to remove your leaderboard entry or have any concerns about your data, you can contact us at politrons@gmail.com and we will assist you promptly. You can also manage your Meta account data directly through your Meta account settings.

7) Children

This game is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe any personal information has been provided to us, please contact us and we will help resolve it.

8) Security

Because we do not collect or transmit personal data, there is no server-side storage of your information. Device-local data security depends on the headset and the user’s device settings.

9) VR Comfort

We aim to minimize discomfort, but VR comfort varies by person. Stop playing if you feel unwell, and consult platform comfort guidelines.

10) Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the Effective date above and publish the revised policy on this page.
